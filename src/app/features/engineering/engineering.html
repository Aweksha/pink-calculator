Conversions, rotations and frame transforms for physics and robotics coursework.

Conventions

  • Angles below are entered in {{ settings.angleMode() === 'DEG' ? 'degrees' : 'radians' }} (shared with the calculator's DEG/RAD setting).
  • Right-handed frames; positive angles rotate counter-clockwise looking down the axis toward the origin.
  • Rotations are active and act on column vectors: p′ = R·p.
  • TAB = [R t; 0 1] maps coordinates in frame B into frame A, so TAC = TAB · TBC.
